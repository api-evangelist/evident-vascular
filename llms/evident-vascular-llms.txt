# Evident Vascular

> Evident Vascular, Inc. is a San Jose, California medical device company developing the Evident Vascular Guidance System, an AI-powered intravascular ultrasound (IVUS) platform purpose-built for peripheral vascular image-guided therapy, with future capability intended for coronary procedures. The company is pre-commercial and works toward FDA 510(k) clearance ahead of a U.S. launch.
